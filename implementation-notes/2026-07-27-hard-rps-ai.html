Algorithm

  1. Score predictors from only prior rounds: global frequency, recency-weighted frequency, first-order Markov, second-order Markov, outcome response, and anti-repeat traps.
  2. Convert the strongest predicted human move into the AI move with counter(predictedMove).
  3. Inject small seeded exploration when confidence is low or when the player appears uniformly random.
  4. Show confidence, model weights, and next-move probabilities after each round.

UI layout

  1. Game table: first-to-7 match, three large fixed-size throw buttons, score strip, pressure panel, and conditional round log.
  2. Explainer: paper-style prose, live probability diagram, equation terms with click definitions, and references.

Decisions

IDChoice · Rationale
D-001Use static HTML/CSS/JS. Empty repo needs no framework or build step.
D-002Make the AI predictive, not omniscient. Fairness depends on reading only completed rounds.
D-003Expose model diagnostics in the game UI. Tough opponents feel fairer when the player can audit the signal.
D-004Move detailed diagnostics out of the play surface. The explainer page already carries probability and model mechanics.
D-005Use first-to-7 as the core loop. A race creates stakes without adding rule complexity.
D-006Show only player and AI in the score strip. The target already appears in the match chip.
D-007Lock the AI move before a short reveal delay. Suspense must not let the AI inspect the clicked move after choosing.

Tradeoffs

IDPicked over Rejected · Reason
T-001ensemble heuristics over trained model. Browser-only play needs deterministic, inspectable behavior.
T-002paper explainer page over modal help. The user asked for a separate explanation page.
T-003pressure HUD over probability bars. The play page should feel like a match, not a model debugger.

Definition of done

  • Game is playable without a build step.
  • AI has no access to the current human throw before choosing.
  • Explainer page shows mechanics, terms, references, and a live control.
  • Desktop and mobile smoke checks pass.
  • Browser assertions report no console errors and no 390px horizontal overflow.
  • Design audit removes dead initial trace, random throw button, hero explanation copy, and play-page probability bars.
  • Clicking a throw shows a short thinking animation before score and AI move reveal.
